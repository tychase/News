Federal grid planners said this summer will be the first broad operational test of transmission hardening projects approved after the previous two years of outage reviews.

Regional operators are staging additional reserve capacity and running coordinated drills with utilities to test contingency playbooks for heat and storm scenarios.

Analysts cautioned that weather volatility still creates downside risk, but said recent infrastructure upgrades should reduce the likelihood of prolonged multi-state service interruptions.
